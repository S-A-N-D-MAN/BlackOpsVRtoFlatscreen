CONTROLS AND GETTING STARTED

These are the right-handed controls. Button names use the Touch layout:
X/Y on the left controller and A/B on the right. Other controller layouts
may use different names for the same primary and secondary buttons.

GETTING STARTED

1. Keep all files and subfolders together inside the extracted BlackOpsVR
   folder. The included .NET files are required by the launcher.
2. Open BlackOpsVR.exe from that extracted folder.
3. Select your Steam installation of Call of Duty: Black Ops.
4. Choose HMD- or controller-oriented movement, turning, reload, grenade
   and rendering-resolution options.
5. Click Install / Update, then Launch VR.

Black Ops VR uses OpenXR. SteamVR works when it is the active 32-bit OpenXR
runtime. COD4 VR's "OpenVR safe controls" preset is part of COD4's legacy
OpenVR fallback and is not used here.

The launcher keeps its private runtime under %LOCALAPPDATA%\BlackOpsVR.
It copies your existing saves locally on first setup. VR progress then
remains separate from your Steam progress; the Steam installation and
its original saves are left untouched. Later launches keep your VR saves.

Open Zombies through the Zombies entry in the single-player menu.
Multiplayer is not supported by this beta.

After loading, resuming, or recovering tracking, briefly release the
buttons and center the sticks before pressing them again. A control held
through a pause or tracking loss may need to be released and re-pressed.

OPTIONS

Rendering resolution:
  Performance: 1280x1440 per eye (2560x1440 packed).
  Standard, the default: 1920x2160 per eye (3840x2160 packed).
  High: 2560x2880 per eye (5120x2880 packed).
  Save and close the game, choose a preset, then select Install / Update
  before launching. These are internal game rendering sizes, separate from
  the OpenXR runtime's headset resolution. Higher settings use more GPU
  resources; no specific frame rate is guaranteed.

Movement orientation:
  HMD oriented: pushing the left stick forward moves in the horizontal
  direction your headset is facing. While moving, deliberate head turns
  also turn the player body. Small head movements stay in headset tracking
  instead of repeatedly shifting the body.
  Controller oriented: pushing the left stick forward moves in the
  horizontal direction the controller holding the movement stick is
  pointing. With this release, this is the left controller.

Room-scale movement:
  During supported ordinary on-foot play, deliberate physical movement
  makes the player body follow your headset position. Small leaning motions
  remain available. Native movement and collision rules still apply; the
  camera is constrained near supported solid surfaces. This is separate
  from the HMD/controller option, which chooses the movement-stick direction.
  Vehicles, ladders, swimming and scripted scenes retain their own controls.
  Tracking jumps reset the horizontal tracking baseline rather than asking
  the character to walk that distance. No extra button is required.

Turning:
  Snap: right stick left/right turns in 45-degree steps. Center the stick
  between steps.
  Smooth: right stick left/right turns continuously at up to 90 degrees
  per second.

Reloading:
  Manual: use the physical reload sequence below.
  Automatic/native: A performs the game's normal button reload, and the
  game can reload an empty weapon automatically. Scripted reload locks
  still apply. This mode also leaves normal cycling/charging to the game.

Grenades:
  Motion is the default: grab at a hip, move your hand, and release.
  Buttons: with left grip released, press the left trigger anywhere to
  prepare a frag. For a tactical grenade, hold left-stick click before
  pressing the left trigger. Hold the trigger to retain/cook, then release
  to throw in your headset direction. Native grenade behavior and available
  ammo apply.

For two-handed aiming, hold left grip with the left hand ahead of the
right hand. Bring the supported gun up near your head to aim down its
sights; lower it to stop aiming down sights. Left trigger is not the
ordinary aim-down-sights button.

When a primary knife is equipped, swing the right controller deliberately
to attack. Slow down between swings to rearm the gesture. Right trigger
does not attack with that primary knife; guns keep their normal trigger.

If you keep the firing trigger held through a melee transition, release it
and press again to resume firing. You do not need to release right grip to
recover ordinary weapon aiming after that melee.

Dual pistols:
  Aim each pistol with its own controller. Right trigger fires the right
  pistol; left trigger fires the left. Do not use the rifle support-hand
  grip pose to aim the pair. Manual reload still uses one shared sequence
  for both pistols: right A, left-hand hip pickup and receiver insertion,
  then any required charging. Independent reloads for each pistol are not
  required. Automatic/native reload remains available in the launcher.

Magnified weapons use a physical view on the weapon's own optic instead
of the PC full-screen scope. Hold left grip for support and bring the optic
to your eye. Sniper optics use 4x and compact optics use 2.5x magnification.
The surrounding world remains visible. Supported thermal optics, including
Rebirth's Enfield, show thermal imagery inside the physical lens while the
surrounding view keeps its normal colors. Variable-zoom optics use fixed 4x;
see KNOWN-ISSUES.txt.

Right grip alone selects the weapon's available alternate; do not hold Y
with it. Release and squeeze again to switch back. Weapons without an
available alternate do nothing. Selection can be blocked while a reload
or grenade is using the left hand.

MANUAL RELOAD

1. Press right A to begin a reload when the weapon needs ammo and you
   have suitable reserve ammo.
2. Move your left hand to your left hip. Press left grip to grab the
   replacement magazine, belt, round, shell batch or tank.
3. Move it into the weapon's receiver and release left grip to insert.
4. If charging is required, hold left trigger at the charging handle,
   pull it back, then release the trigger.

You can reach the charging handle with the trigger already held, but you
still need to make the full pull before releasing it. Not every weapon
needs charging after an insertion.

Pump actions:
  Use left grip at the pump, pull back, then push forward while keeping
  the grip held. Keep the hand along the pump's axis. Pump after a shot
  when the weapon requires it; simply holding the fore-end does not cycle
  the action.

Tube-fed weapons and internal magazines:
  Repeat the hip-to-receiver insertion. Press A again, with no round held,
  to finish a partially loaded tube/chamber. Feeding also finishes when
  full or out of reserve ammo. Complete any required pump/charge afterward.

Other mechanisms:
  Break-action weapons follow the game's shell batch size. Launchers and
  crossbows use their appropriate receiver rather than a universal rifle
  reload.
  Supported dual-wield pairs use one physical sequence for both guns.
  Fuel tanks use an exchange and may discard fuel left in the old tank.

Capacity, reserve ammo and native partial-reload restrictions still
matter. Firing can remain blocked until a required reload or cycle is
complete. The guides/hand positions are still being refined, and not every
weapon variant has been individually checked.

Crossbow:
  Aim with the right controller and hold left grip for two-hand support.
  Press A to start the manual reload. Take a bolt from the left hip with
  left grip, move it to the bow's receiver, and release grip to insert.
  Hold left trigger at the charging/string area, pull backward about 9 cm,
  and release to finish cocking. The game retains the bolt's native flight
  and gravity. Automatic/native reload remains an alternative in the launcher.

  In Executive Order's zipline phase, aim the rope bolt at the marked
  anchor and press right trigger. The mission intentionally locks movement
  and rejects firing away from the anchor until that shot starts the zipline.

GRENADES: MOTION MODE (DEFAULT)

Use the left hand and left trigger:
  Left hip: frag.
  Right hip: tactical grenade, reached with the left hand.

Release left grip before grabbing. Press the left trigger at the desired
hip, hold it to retain/cook, then make a throwing motion and release the
trigger. The native textured grenade is held by the floating left glove;
recent hand movement determines the throw.

GRENADES: BUTTONS MODE

With left grip released, press the left trigger anywhere for a frag.
To select a tactical grenade, hold left-stick click before pressing the
trigger. Hold to retain/cook;
release to throw toward your headset's viewing direction. You do not
need to reach a hip or make a throwing motion.

Preparing a button grenade releases the tactical modifier's sprint action
while the trigger belongs to that grenade.

New button-grenade grabs are blocked during a manual reload, while right
A or left grip is held, while supporting the weapon, and while dual
wielding. Release the conflicting control before a fresh grenade press.
An otherwise valid grenade press with no ammo does not turn into a jump.
Holding left grip deliberately keeps the ordinary left-trigger jump
instead of grabbing a grenade. When dual wielding, left trigger continues
to fire the left gun. Right stick up also jumps in Buttons mode, including
while holding a button grenade; a physical reload still blocks jumping.

SCRIPTED SCENES AND MOUNTED WEAPONS

For use prompts or repeated-tap sequences, use left X. Frozen cinematic
sections still follow the game's rules; they do not all allow weapon
control just because a gun appears on screen.

At the final Dragovich grab prompt labeled "press left mouse", release
right trigger once and press it again. This supplies the scripted attack
button even when the mission has disabled normal weapons and movement;
it does not make that cinematic a freely aimed weapon sequence.

At the Vorkuta train-jump Space prompt, press left trigger or push the
right stick straight up. Left-stick forward is movement, not jump.

Scripted car controls:
  Left trigger: hold the game's right-mouse/ADS action to reverse.
  Right trigger: hold the left-mouse/attack action to accelerate.
  Left X: use/interact.

These mappings apply to the linked scripted context, not every vehicle.
Analog left-stick movement remains available for linked carry sequences.
Ordinary turning, weapon cycling, stance and reload actions are not added
to those weapon-disabled scripted sections.

Admitted mounted weapons:
  Right trigger: fire.
  Left X: use/interact where the game permits it.
  Aim with the right controller; native turret limits still apply.
  Your headset can look independently. Stick aiming/body turn is suppressed
  while using supported fixed guns. Native turret aiming limits still apply.

Anti-air and native vehicle weapons:
  Right trigger: fire the vehicle weapon.
  Left X: use/interact where the game permits it.

Release the trigger once when entering or leaving a mounted/vehicle weapon.
This prevents a held trigger from carrying into the new control context.

VORKUTA SLINGSHOT

Rotate the right controller to aim relative to the pose when you take
control. Hold the left stick down to pull the sling, then release the stick
to fire. The game's aiming limits and shot trajectory remain active.

VORKUTA MOTORCYCLE AND TRUCK

Motorcycle:
  Left stick forward/back: drive/reverse.
  Left stick left/right: steer.
  Right controller: aim the shotgun.
  Right trigger: fire the shotgun.
  Right A: native handbrake.
  Right grip: native reload command.
  Left X: interact when prompted.

The motorcycle retains its scripted shotgun animation and reload sequence.
The drive stick can resume while held after a short tracking interruption;
release and press the trigger again to resume firing after an interruption.

Truck machine gun:
  Rotate the right controller to aim within the turret's limits.
  Right trigger fires; left X interacts where the game permits.

GUIDED MISSILE LAUNCHER

Aim and fire with the right controller and right trigger. Once the missile
is flying, rotate the controller away from its launch orientation to steer
up/down and left/right. The right stick is an alternative steering control;
center it first if it was held during launch.

Release the launch trigger, then press it again for native manual detonation.
Normal walking and turning controls are suspended during missile guidance.
The optional boost remains the native keyboard 4 action; no VR boost button
has been added in this beta.

A and B together cancel the altitude input. Helicopter weapons follow
the headset target separately from flight steering. The turret still
has native turning speed and projectile behavior, so it may take a moment
to catch up. These are pilot controls, not an eject-seat binding or a

Redemption's opening approach flies automatically. Sticks and A/B become
flight controls only when the mission hands over control. Weapons become
available when the mission enables them. Center the sticks and release
buttons at that handoff before using them again.

Rockets fire in pairs at the mission's roughly one-second interval and
do not require manual reload. The miniguns can overheat; release the trigger
and let them cool. The optional PC sight-zoom toggle is not mapped in VR.


AIR SUPPORT RADIO

When the mission makes support available, start with the left stick centered,
hold left Y, then push the left stick left. Release Y and center the stick.
Aim the right controller at an eligible target and press right trigger to
confirm. An ordinary Y tap still switches weapons; while support selection
is available, that tap switches on release. Native cooldown, clear line of
sight and mission target restrictions apply. At Hue City's final fuel-air-
bomb call, target the designated tank area, not arbitrary distant infantry.

KHE SANH TRUCK TOW

Aim with the right controller; press right trigger to fire, then release it.
During flight, turn/tilt the right controller to steer the missile, or use
the right stick. Guidance ends under the game's normal rules. The green
scoped presentation remains. The launcher is available again when the
mission permits it. No additional boost control has been added.

LADDERS AND SWIMMING

Approach a ladder and hold left stick up to climb; center it to stop or
pull down to descend. Looking around while attached does not redirect you
away from the ladder. Continue up to get off at the top.

Underwater, hold left stick forward and look where you want to swim:
look up to rise, down to dive, and sideways to change heading. Pull back
to swim in the opposite direction; sideways stick movement stays horizontal.
Swimming follows the headset even with controller-oriented ground movement.
Turning remains available during the scripted tree/boat approach. Stay
underwater during the stealth approach; beside the boat, use a deliberate
right-controller knife swing or right-stick click for the takedown.
The game's detection and timing requirements still apply.

SCRIPTED MELEE, TUNNEL ESCAPE AND DOORS

Right-stick click supplies the PC V/melee action at scripted prompts,
including the tunnel rock barriers and Rebirth ladder guard. These prompts
do not all accept a knife swing.

At the tunnel exit door, briefly hold X. Center and reapply the left stick
after the transition, push forward to crawl, and click the right stick at
each dig prompt. The scripted crawl keeps its original animated arms/camera.

For look-sensitive doors, look at the door with the headset and briefly
hold X. This is separate from controller pointing in menus. Rebirth's
opening hands/hatchet retain their scripted placement and animations.

REBIRTH BTR GUNNER

Rotate the right controller to aim the turret. Hold right trigger for its
machine gun/cannon. Use left trigger for the vehicle grenade launcher.
The mission drives this vehicle automatically.

REBIRTH THERMAL ENFIELD AND STRELA

Thermal optic: hold left grip for support and bring the optic close to an
eye. Thermal imagery and actor silhouettes appear inside the lens only.
Lower the weapon, release support or switch weapons to stop using it.

Strela: hold left grip with the support hand ahead of the right hand.
Point the right controller at an eligible helicopter and hold steady
until the native lock-on tone/indicator, then press right trigger to fire.
The rifle's near-head shouldering pose is not required to request lock-on.
Native target, obstruction and lock timing rules still apply; without a
valid lock the game can refuse the shot. Reload with the selected mode.
In manual mode, press A, take ammunition from the left hip with left grip,
insert it at the receiver and complete any required cycling.
